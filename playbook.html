PHASE 1  ·  Day 1–7
Point (Split Action)
▼
01 Point Elbow Drill ▼

Player 1 passes to Player 5 at the elbow then sprints to screen for the wing. Wing cuts off the screen with 4 options.

Steps
  1. Player 1 passes to Player 5 at the elbow
  2. Player 1 sprints to set a screen for the wing player
  3. Player 5 looks for the wing cutting off the screen
  4. Player 1 steps back — open for 3pt
  5. Player 5 also open at elbow for mid-range
Cut Options
CutWhenResult
UnderDefender goes over screenPass from 5 → layup
ContinueDefender fights hardFar corner → 3pt
CurlDefender goes underWrap tight → catch from 5
SlipDefender anticipatesPlayer 5 cuts → layup
Key
3 players per rep. Every player gets a shot. Build each cut then switch sides.

02 Point Same Drill ▼

Same as Point Elbow but Player 1 screens for the corner player on the same side instead of the wing.

Steps
  1. Player 1 passes to Player 5 and sprints to screen for corner player
  2. Corner player cuts off screen — same 4 read options
  3. Screener steps back for 3pt from coach
  4. Player 5 receives pass from coach for elbow shot
Cut Options
Under · Continue · Curl · Slip — same 4 reads as Play 1
▶ YouTube princeton offense corner screen
03 Rub Drill ▼

Player 4 runs close to Player 5 at the elbow to use them as a brush screen — cutting to opposite corner. Players do not touch.

Steps
  1. Player 4 rubs through to opposite corner brushing inside Player 5's elbow
  2. Player 5 sets brush screen at the elbow — rubs opposite elbow
  3. Player 4 catches pass from coach — 3pt shot
  4. Player 1 passes to Player 5 at elbow — elbow jump shot
Key Rule
Rub guard inside elbow. Rub Player 5 outside elbow. Players do NOT touch — it is a legal brush screen.
▶ YouTube princeton offense rub cut brush screen
04 Rub Point ▼

Connects the Rub Drill to the full Point formation starting in 4-out 1-in alignment.

Steps
  1. Start in 4-out 1-in alignment
  2. Player 4 rubs through corner using Player 5 as brush screen
  3. Player 2 lifts to wing position
  4. Player 1 now screens — choice is Elbow or Same side
▶ YouTube princeton offense 4-out 1-in rub action
05 Hand-Off Point Elbow ▼

Player 1 dribbles at Player 3 and makes a short pitch — creating a two-man game at the elbow with 6 read options.

Steps
  1. Player 1 dribbles toward Player 3
  2. Player 3 lifts to receive a short pitch from Player 1 — not a full handoff
  3. Player 1 continues cut to corner — Players 2 and 4 exchange weak side
  4. Player 3 passes to Player 4 — rubs through to opposite corner
  5. Player 5 pops to opposite elbow — 6 read options begin
6 Reads
ReadAction
SlipPlayer 5 cuts to basket — pass for layup
OverGo over screen → shot
UnderCut under screen → layup from 5
Step BackStep back → pull-up
CurlCurl around screener → catch and shoot
ContinueCut to opposite corner → 3pt
Key
Make a SHORT PITCH — not a full handoff. Creates spacing and avoids charging fouls. This is the Point Series.

06 Hand-Off Point Same ▼

Same hand-off concept as Play 5 but Player 5 screens on the same side. All 6 reads still apply.

Steps
  1. Player 1 dribbles at Player 3 — same side pitch
  2. Player 3 receives pitch — rubs to opposite corner with brush from 5
  3. Player 5 pops to same-side elbow
  4. Player 4 reads — same 6 options as Play 5

07 1 Pass Wing Entry ▼

Enter the Princeton action with one pass — give and go — flowing into Point action.

Movement 1 — Give and Go
  1. Player 1 passes to wing Player 3 — Cheetah cut (give-and-go) to basket
  2. If Player 1 not open — continue cut to opposite corner
  3. Player 4 lifts to wing — Player 2 fills to basket line
Movement 2 — Flow into Point
  1. Player 3 passes to Player 2 — drifts to corner for spacing
  2. Player 5 pops to ball-side elbow — triggers Point Elbow or Same
Look-Away Options
OptionAction
Look-away 1Player 5 looks away → dribbles at 3 → Bingo back cut for layup
Look-away 2Player 5 looks away → dribbles at 3 → DHO → Player 3 dives for layup
Key
The pass to Player 2 triggers Point Elbow or Same. Know it automatically.
▶ YouTube princeton offense wing entry give and go
08 1 Dribble Wing Entry ▼

Instead of passing, Player 1 dribbles at the wing to force the back cut or reset.

Steps
  1. Player 1 dribbles AT Player 3 — Dribble-at action
  2. Player 3 reads defender — back cut for layup from Player 1
  3. If back cut not open — Player 3 continues cut to ball-side corner
  4. Or Player 3 retreats dribble to alley for spacing — Player 4 fills opposite
  5. Player 3 passes to Player 4 — rubs through — flows into Point Elbow or Same
Key
Dribble at the butt of the defender. Back cut must be 10-2 stance toward the ball — explode 0 to 60 instantly. Any step back or over triggers Point action.

Low Post Entry

09 Low Post Entry ▼

Pass into the low post to create inside-outside balance. Passer cuts through for a layup.

Steps
  1. Player 3 passes to Player 5 at low post — cuts straight through middle for layup
  2. If Player 3 doesn't get pass — continues to opposite corner
  3. Player 4 fills to wing — Player 2 fills basket line — Player 1 fills ball-side corner
  4. Player 5 passes out to Player 2 — immediately pops to same-side elbow
  5. Flows into Point Elbow or Same
Key
Pass to low post often — creates inside-outside balance. Ball screen = DDM.
▶ YouTube princeton offense low post entry split action
10 Split Action ▼

After the ball goes to Player 5 in the post — the passer becomes the screener.

Key Rule
The player who makes the post entry from the wing is ALWAYS the screener in split action.
Steps
  1. Player 3 passes to Player 5 — becomes the screener at the elbow
  2. Player 3 screens for Player 1 at the elbow
  3. Player 1 reads the screen — 3 options
Options
OptionAction
OverGo over screen → shot from 5
UnderUnder cut → layup from 5 → screener steps back
Over + lookGo over → 5 also looks for on-the-cut pass

PHASE 4  ·  Day 16–21
Return and Jungle
▼
11 Dribble-at Drill ▼

Building the back cut trigger under pressure.

Steps
  1. Player 1 passes to Player 2
  2. Player 2 rips through and dribbles AT Player 3's defender
  3. Player 3 Bingo back cuts for layup from Player 2
  4. After — Players 1 and 2 each get 3pt shots from coach
Key
Dribble at the butt of the defender. Back cut is at 10-2 stance toward ball — must go from 0 to 60mph instantly.
▶ YouTube princeton offense dribble-at bingo back cut
12 Point Return ▼

What to do when Player 5 is denied by the defense.

Option 1 — Bingo
  1. Player 5 is denied
  2. Player 1 does spin-back dribble
  3. Player 3 does Bingo (back cut) to basket for layup
  4. Player 2 lifts to wing if Player 3 not open
Option 2 — Buzz
  1. Player 5 still denied
  2. Player 1 spin-back dribble
  3. Player 2 does Buzz (cut to wing) — receives pass from Player 1
  4. Player 4 lifts — offense resets
Jungle Wrinkle
  1. Player 5 steps out toward Player 2
  2. Player 2 passes to Player 5 — Player 5 now has the ball outside
  3. Player 2 now has 2 options — Same (Rhino) or Elbow (Tiger)

13 Chin ▼

Continuity action where Player 4 does a Chin cut off Player 5's back screen.

Steps
  1. Player 1 pitches to Player 4 — DHO action at elbow area
  2. Players 2 and 3 exchange on weak side
  3. Player 3 swings ball to Player 4 — Player 4 chin cuts
  4. Player 4 cuts to basket off Player 5's back screen
Options
SituationAction
Option 1 — Player 4 gets passScores. Player 1 gets swing → Point action
Option 2 — Player 4 doesn't get passContinues corner. Player 2 lifts. Player 3 gets flare screen from 5
Chin Flow — flare not openPlayer 3 dribbles out → Player 2 passes to 1 → Player 5 back screen for 2 → continuity

14 Chin Jungle ▼

Chin action combined with the Jungle wrinkle — the most advanced action in the system.

Steps
  1. Player 1 pitches to Player 4 — Player 4 Chin cuts
  2. Player 4 passes to Player 3 — Player 5 sets back screen for Player 4
  3. Player 4 chin cuts to basket — Player 2 lifts — Player 3 can pass to 4
  4. If flare not open — Player 3 continues cut — Player 5 looks for pass at elbow — Player 1 fills
2-Man Game Options
OptionAction
Give and GoPass to 5 → Cheetah cut
Pin DownPlayer 2 sets for 4 → 4 goes under or curl
OverPlayer 4 goes over → shot → Player 2 slips to rim
Tiger ScreenPlayer 2 goes over top of 5 → Player 1 can under curl or over
Key
Any step back or over to 2-man game triggers DDM. This flows back into itself — it never stops until a shot is taken.
