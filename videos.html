Skill-Specific Drill Videos
Catch and Shoot Off Screens ▼
What to Look For
  • Feet already in shooting stance before catching the ball
  • Eyes finding the rim on the cut — not after the catch
  • Zero wasted motion from catch to release
Your Drill
  1. Set up at wing — imagine a screener at the elbow
  2. Start behind the 3-point line — V-cut to use the screen
  3. Curl or fade depending on where the "defender" goes
  4. Catch in shooting stance — no reset needed
  5. 10 makes each side — game speed only
YTCatch and Shoot Off Screens Drill
V-Cut and L-Cut Off-Ball Movement ▼
What to Look For
  • The fake step in the wrong direction before the cut
  • Full sprint speed on the actual cut — 0 to 60, no jogging
  • Hands ready and target shown before ball arrives
Your Drill
  1. Start at the 3-point line — take 2 steps toward the basket
  2. Plant hard on outside foot — explode back to the wing (V-cut)
  3. Or plant and change direction at 90 degrees (L-cut)
  4. Catch in stride — shoot immediately — 15 makes each side
YTV-Cut and L-Cut Off-Ball Movement Drill
On-Ball Defense and Closeouts ▼
What to Look For
  • Sprint to closeout then brake hard — not a lazy jog
  • High hands on the contest — don't foul
  • Stay low and balanced on the recovery
Your Drill
  1. Start under basket — pass thrown to the wing
  2. Sprint full speed to the ball handler
  3. Brake at 2 steps — high hands — don't fly by
  4. Force baseline or force middle depending on scout
YTOn-Ball Defense and Closeout Drill
Pull-Up Mid-Range ▼
What to Look For
  • The one dribble gather — not two or three
  • Jump straight up — not fading unless the defender is on you
  • Same release as your catch-and-shoot — don't rush it
Your Drill
  1. Start at the wing with ball — 1 hard dribble at the elbow
  2. Gather in one step — jump straight up
  3. Same shooting motion every single rep
  4. 10 makes each side — no exceptions
YTPull-Up Mid-Range Jump Shot Drill
2-Ball Dribbling ▼
10-Minute Daily Routine
  1. Simultaneous bounces — stationary low — 1 min
  2. Alternating bounces — stationary — 1 min
  3. Simultaneous crossover — stationary — 1 min
  4. Walk full court — both balls bouncing simultaneously — 3×
  5. One hard dribble — both balls — then freeze and reset — 2 min
Do this EVERY day. It will transform your weak hand in 30 days.

Film Study Routines
🌙 Daily — 10 to 15 Minutes
  1. Pick one player from your model 3 — any recent game
  2. Watch with one specific question: "What does she do off-ball?"
  3. Pause every time she moves without the ball
  4. Notice where the defender is BEFORE the cut
  5. Write 1 thing in your journal after
📅 Weekly Saturday — 30 Minutes
  1. Watch 1 full quarter of a D1 women's game — track only the SG
  2. Note every cut she makes — correct or incorrect read?
  3. Write down the 2 best decisions she made
  4. Write down 1 mistake you would not repeat
  5. Set 1 specific film goal for next week
📹 Monthly Self-Film Review
  1. Film yourself in a full practice or game
  2. Watch only your off-ball movement — ignore the ball
  3. Are you in the right spot at the right time?
  4. Write 3 specific things to improve next month
  5. Compare to the same drill 1 month ago
