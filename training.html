Shooting Program
🎯
SHOOTING PROGRAM 500 Shot Workout
▼
50 Form Shots
  • 5 spots, 10 shots each, 3–5 feet from basket
  • Focus on mechanics, balance, and follow-through
  • Keep elbow under ball, hold follow-through, aim for clean arc and soft touch
100 Catch and Shoot Jumpers
  • 20 shots from each spot: corner, wing, top, opposite wing, opposite corner
  • Inside the 3-point line
  • Focus on catching in a ready position, quick footwork, and shooting immediately
100 Off-the-Dribble Shots
  • 25 one-dribble pull-ups going right
  • 25 one-dribble pull-ups going left
  • 25 crossover pull-ups
  • 25 step-backs or hesitation pull-ups
  • Different spots on the floor
  • Emphasize balance and rising straight up into your shot
50 Game Shots
  • 10 corner threes right side
  • 10 corner threes left side
  • 10 wing threes right side
  • 10 wing threes left side
  • 10 transition threes — catch while moving into the shot
100 Movement Shots
  • 20 shots relocating from corner to wing
  • 20 shots relocating from wing to corner
  • 20 shots coming off an imaginary down screen
  • 20 shots sprinting into transition threes
  • 20 quick catch-and-shoot after changing direction
100 Free Throws
  • Break into 10 sets of 10
  • Focus on routine, breathing, and consistency especially while tired
  • Every miss is 1 full court jog or sprint
500 SHOTS TOTAL
Track your makes. Know your percentage. Improve every session.
