When a Slump Starts — Do This
TRIGGER
Recognize it without panic. "I'm in a slump. My anchor kicks in now."
STEP 1
Fire the anchor — 💨 👏 "Next"
STEP 2
Shoulders back. Head up. Clap. Sprint back on defense.
STEP 3
One Job — pick ONE thing you fully control this possession.
STEP 4
Find your easiest shot — the V-cut catch and shoot. Not a hero shot.
STEP 5
Impact without scoring — screen, deflection, pass, charge.
Unbreakable
01 The Next Shot Rule ▼
"One second to think about a miss. Then it is gone permanently."
Say "next" out loud and get into V-cut or defensive stance immediately after every miss in practice. Train it until it's automatic.
02 The Body Language Rule ▼
"Shoulders back. Head up. Sprint. No matter what."
D1 coaches and scouts watch body language constantly. A player who misses 3 shots and still sprints back is a player they want.
03 The Process Rule ▼
"Good V-cut + good shot selection = success. Make or miss is secondary."
After each quarter count correct cuts taken versus forced shots — not points scored.
04 The One Job Rule ▼
"When forcing — shrink it to ONE specific job this possession."
Examples: My job is to run a perfect V-cut. My job is to set a screen. My job is to be in the right spot.
05 The Amnesia Rule ▼
"Bad game — review once — delete. Good game — replay in vivid detail."
Your brain strengthens whatever pathway you replay. Replay success, not failure.
