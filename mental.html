Root Cause
Right now each shot feels important and separate. Elite players treat each shot as meaningless — the only shot that matters is the next one. This is trainable. You train it the same way you train your V-cut — with reps.
5 Unbreakable Rules
01 The Next Shot Rule ▼
"After every miss you have exactly 1 second to think about it. Then it is gone permanently."
Say "next" out loud and get into V-cut stance or defensive stance immediately after every miss in practice. Every single time. This is how it becomes automatic in games.
02 The Body Language Rule ▼
"Shoulders back. Head up. Sprint back on defense. No matter what just happened."
D1 coaches and scouts watch body language constantly. A player who misses 3 shots and still sprints back with their head up is a player they want to recruit. This is free — it costs zero talent and zero physical ability.
03 The Process Rule ▼
"Good V-cut + good shot selection = success. Make or miss is secondary."
After each quarter, count correct cuts taken versus forced shots — not points scored. A perfect V-cut that leads to a miss is still a success. A forced shot that somehow goes in is still a failure of process.
04 The One Job Rule ▼
"When forcing — shrink it to ONE specific job this possession."
  • My job is to run a perfect V-cut
  • My job is to set a hard screen
  • My job is to be in the right spot
  • My job is to sprint back on defense
One job is achievable. Five jobs create overwhelm. When you feel yourself forcing — shrink.
05 The Amnesia Rule ▼
"Bad game — review once — delete permanently. Good game — replay in vivid detail."
Your brain strengthens whatever neural pathway you replay. If you replay a missed shot 50 times before sleep, you practice missing. Replay your best shot 50 times and you practice making it. This is literal neuroscience.

Pre-Game Routine
🌅 Morning of Game — 5 Minutes
  1. Sit still for 2 minutes — just breathe, no phone
  2. Visualize yourself making 3 shots in slow motion — feel the catch, feel the release
  3. Say all 4 mantras out loud — not in your head, out loud
  4. Set ONE intention — not a stat — something like "I will run every cut at full speed"
  5. Eat a protein breakfast — you cannot perform on empty
🏀 Warm-Up — Same Order Every Game
  1. Form shooting at 5 feet — 10 makes
  2. Elbow mid-range — 5 makes each side
  3. V-cut → catch → shoot — 5 makes each side
  4. 3-point spots — 3 spots, 3 makes each
  5. Free throws — 10, always finish on a make
⏱️ Final 5 Minutes Before Tip
  1. Find a quiet spot — even the bathroom works
  2. One 4-4-4 breath cycle — inhale 4, hold 4, exhale 4
  3. Say your ONE intention for the game out loud
  4. Remind yourself: slumps have a protocol — you are never helpless
  5. Headphones in, same pre-game playlist

What the Legends Say
Kobe Bryant
"I have self-doubt. I have insecurity. I have fear of failure. We all have self-doubt. You don't deny it — but you also don't capitulate to it."
The Lesson
Even Kobe felt it. The slump feeling is completely normal — capitulating to it is the choice. The anchor is how you refuse to capitulate.
Kevin Durant
"Shooting slumps are normal. I missed nine shots in a row once and took the tenth one anyway. You have to have the mentality that the next one is going in — every single time."
The Lesson
He didn't stop shooting after 9 misses. You don't stop running your V-cut after 2 misses. The process continues regardless of the result.
Michael Jordan
"I never looked at the consequences of missing a big shot. Because when you think about the consequences you always think of a negative result."
The Lesson
When you force shots during a slump you are thinking about consequences. Think process. Not consequences. Good process — the result takes care of itself.
Kobe Bryant
"Confidence isn't something you have. It's something you create."
The Lesson
You create confidence through your routine, your mantras, your body language, and your process. It is not given to you. You build it. Every day.
Carmelo Anthony
"The mental is to the physical as four is to one."
The Lesson
Your pull-up is already there physically. The mental side is what unlocks it in games. Four times more important. That is why this page exists.
