For the Hard Days — Tap to Read
When you miss 3 in a row ▼
Three misses is not a slump. Three misses is Tuesday. Steph Curry misses three in a row in almost every game — then makes the next five. You have a protocol. It exists for exactly this moment. Exhale. Clap. "Next." That is all. Do not change your shot, do not change your mindset, do not panic. The next one is going in. It always does.
When you feel like you are not improving ▼
Improvement is not linear. It looks like a flat line for weeks, then a sudden jump. Every rep you are putting in right now is loading that jump. Read your journal entries from 4 weeks ago — you will see the improvement even when you cannot feel it. Trust the process. Trust the reps. They do not disappear.
When you compare yourself to others ▼
You are watching someone else's highlight reel and comparing it to your behind-the-scenes. You do not see their doubts, their misses, their bad practices. You are not competing with them — you are competing with who you were yesterday. That is the only comparison that matters.
When practice feels pointless ▼
The reps you put in when you do not feel like it are worth more than the reps you put in when you do. That is when character is built. Every D1 player you watch has had a thousand practices exactly like the one you are dreading right now — and they went anyway. So do you.
When you feel the panic coming ▼
You have a protocol. You are not helpless. The panic is not you — it is just your amygdala doing what amygdalas do. You know what to do: exhale, clap, "next." You have practiced this. It works. The feeling will pass in under 3 seconds if you use your anchor. Use it.
When you wonder if D1 is really possible ▼
You are in 9th grade with a system, a plan, a playbook, a training schedule, model players to study, and a mental toughness protocol. Most players your age have none of that. The players who make it to D1 are not always the most talented — they are the most systematic and the most consistent. You have both. Keep going.
